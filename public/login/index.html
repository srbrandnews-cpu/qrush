Step 1 of 8

QR Scan karo

Customer table par laga QR code scan karke seedha digital menu open karta hai. Koi app download karne ki zaroorat nahi hoti.

Step 2 of 8

Order Place karo

Apni pasand ki dishes select karke cart mein add karta hai aur "Place Order" par click karke order bhej deta hai.

Step 3 of 8

Kitchen ko Order pahunchta hai

Order turant Kitchen Display par show hota hai. Sound alert ke saath chef ko turant pata chal jata hai.

Step 4 of 8

Preparing start hota hai

Chef order accept karke status "Preparing" par set karta hai. Ab customer apne phone par live status dekh sakta hai.

Step 5 of 8

Khana Ready ho jata hai

Khana taiyar hone par chef "Ready" mark karta hai. Customer ko turant "Order Ready" ka notification milta hai.

Step 6 of 8

Table par Serve hota hai

Waiter/order taker khana table par serve karke status "Served" mark karta hai.

Step 7 of 8

UPI ya Cash se Payment

Customer apne phone se UPI QR/link par pay karta hai ya seedha Cash Counter par cash se payment karta hai.

Step 8 of 8

Cashier par Bill Submit

Cashier payment confirm karke bill submit karta hai aur order complete ho jata hai. Poora flow paperless hota hai.
