How QRush works

Table par QR scan se lekar Payment tak

Har step automate — staff aur customer dono ko clear visibility milti hai

📱

1. QR Scan

Customer table par lage QR code ko scan karke apne phone par digital menu kholta hai. Koi app install nahi chahiye.

🛒

2. Order Place

Customer menu se items select karke cart mein add karta hai aur confirm karke order place kar deta hai.

👨‍🍳

3. Kitchen ko Order Milta Hai

Order turant Kitchen Display par dikhta hai. Chef order accept karke khana banana shuru kar deta hai.

⏱️

4. Preparing → Ready

Jab khana taiyar ho jata hai, kitchen se Ready mark hota hai aur customer ko live status dikhta hai.

🍽️

5. Table Par Serve

Staff khana table par serve karta hai aur order ko Served mark kar deta hai.

💳

6. UPI ya Cash se Payment

Customer apne phone se UPI link par pay kar sakta hai ya Cash Counter par cash se payment kar sakta hai.

✅

7. Cashier par Bill Submit

Cashier bill verify karke payment complete mark karta hai. Poora process paperless aur real-time hota hai.
