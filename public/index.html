How it works

Char steps. Bas.

Order lene se serve karne tak — sab kuch ek hi flow mein, bina paper chits ke.

1

QR scan

Customer table ke QR ko phone se scan karta hai — menu turant khul jata hai. Install ki zaroorat nahi.

2

Order place

Items cart mein daal kar order confirm. Table aur customer ka phone auto-tag ho jata hai.

3

Kitchen & counter

Order live kitchen display aur cash counter dono par ek saath — sound alert ke saath.

4

Ready alert

Ready hote hi customer ke phone par push notification — phir table par serve.
