Frequently asked questions

Jo sabse zyada poocha jaata hai.

Kya QRush sach me free hai?

Haan. Free plan me apni restaurant ka QR menu page, unlimited menu photo pages, QR download/print, scan analytics aur social links — sab free milte hain. Koi card ya payment detail nahi maangi jaati.

Customer ko koi app download karna padega?

Nahi. Customer sirf phone camera se QR scan karega aur menu seedha browser me khul jayegi. iPhone, Android — sab par kaam karta hai.

Menu kaise upload karun?

Apne phone se menu card ki photo khinchein ya PDF chunein. Photos automatically compress ho jaati hain, isliye badi camera photo bhi upload ho jaati hai. Ek se zyada image add kar sakte hain.

QR code kaise print karun?

Dashboard me "Download QR (PNG)" se image download karein, ya "Print QR" se seedha print nikaalein. Table stand, acrylic frame ya menu card par laga dein.

Menu baad me change kar sakta hoon?

Bilkul. Contact number aur password se login karein, "Edit my page" kholein aur naya menu upload karein — customer ko turant naya menu dikhega. QR code same rehta hai.

Password bhool gaya to?

Login box ke neeche "Forgot password?" par click karein. Apna registered contact number aur naya password daal kar reset kar lein.

Kya mujhe online payment karni hogi?

Abhi nahi. Phase 1 me payment gateway active nahi hai — koi button aapse paise nahi maangega. Paid plans sirf propose kiye gaye hain.
