FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything owners ask before creating their first QR menu.

Is QRush really free?

Yes. The Free plan includes your restaurant QR menu page, unlimited menu photo pages, QR download and print, scan analytics and social links. No card or payment details are asked.

Do customers need to download an app?

No. Customers scan the QR with their phone camera and the menu opens directly in the browser — iPhone, Android, any modern phone.

How do I upload my menu?

Take a photo of your menu card or choose a PDF from your phone. Photos are compressed automatically, so large camera files upload fine. You can add more than one image.

How do I print the QR code?

In your dashboard use “Download QR (PNG)” to save the image, or “Print QR” to print directly. Place it on a table stand, acrylic frame or your menu card.

Can I change my menu later?

Yes. Log in with your contact number and password, open “Edit my page” and upload the new menu — customers see it immediately. The QR code stays the same.

What if I forget my password?

Use “Forgot password?” below the login box. Enter your registered contact number and set a new password.

Do I have to pay online right now?

No. Phase 1 has no payment gateway. Starter and Business are proposed plans and no button on this page takes money.
